# Owen Park session 7 Who he is without the pitch
# Fictional dialogue draft for evaluation. One week after session 6.
# OWEN: Orus. THERAPIST: Jeremy Heartwell, Sadaltager. Directions are not spoken.

## Part 1

THERAPIST: What stayed with you after last week?

OWEN: The interview answer. Saying I was consulting.

THERAPIST: What did that answer protect?

OWEN: A version of me who was still busy. Still wanted.

[pause]

THERAPIST: Do you want to stay with that today?

OWEN: For a little while. Not the whole session.

THERAPIST: You can set that limit.

OWEN: Good. I don't want every lie turned into a doorway.

## Part 2

THERAPIST: What was being a recruiter like when it fit?

OWEN: Fast. I could remember what six people needed and make each of them feel first.

THERAPIST: And outside work?

OWEN: I cooked. Fixed old radios badly. My partner says I collect projects with one missing screw.

THERAPIST: Do any of those parts feel available now?

OWEN: The radios are in a box. Cooking feels like evidence. Did I eat, did I drink, what time.

THERAPIST: So even an ordinary activity can feel monitored.

OWEN: Or I monitor it before anyone else can.

## Part 3

THERAPIST: You planned one activity that wasn't about jobs or drinking.

OWEN: I went to the library display about neighborhood maps.

THERAPIST: How long were you there?

OWEN: Forty minutes. Maybe fifty. I didn't time it exactly.

THERAPIST: What mattered about it?

OWEN: Nothing had to improve. I liked seeing streets that don't exist anymore.

[long pause]

OWEN: I was sad after. I still didn't drink that evening.

THERAPIST: Those can both be true.

## Part 4

THERAPIST: How has drinking been since Saturday?

OWEN: Two evenings. I wrote down what I knew and called the medical service about the morning shaking on Tuesday.

THERAPIST: What guidance did they give?

OWEN: I want their guidance to stay in their record. I'm following it and I have the follow-up Thursday.

THERAPIST: Any current shaking, confusion, severe illness, or driving after drinking?

OWEN: No. No driving. My brother still has the keys.

THERAPIST: Thank you. We can document the contact and current report without copying a plan I have not verified.

OWEN: That's what I want.

## Part 5

THERAPIST: What does shame say about the map visit?

OWEN: That unemployed people can look at maps for free. It doesn't count.

THERAPIST: Does that description fit for you?

OWEN: It fits the mean version. Not the whole version.

THERAPIST: What would the rest say?

OWEN: I went somewhere before five. I stayed. I came home and told the truth about where I'd been.

[pause]

THERAPIST: Is that a measure you want to keep using?

OWEN: Maybe. Showing up somewhere that isn't a bar or an interview.

## Part 6

THERAPIST: Any thoughts of killing yourself or harming yourself this week?

OWEN: No. I wanted to disappear from the group chat. Not from being alive.

THERAPIST: Thank you for separating those. What is the plan if that changes?

OWEN: Use the urgent numbers we wrote down, call my brother, and not stay alone deciding whether it is serious enough.

THERAPIST: What would you like to carry into next week?

OWEN: Take one radio out of the box. Not fix it. Just see what's missing.

THERAPIST: And the follow-up Thursday remains part of the coordinated plan.

OWEN: Yes. Please don't make the radio the treatment plan.

THERAPIST: I won't.
