# Owen Park session 8 The lapse he almost omitted
# Fictional dialogue draft for evaluation. One week after session 7.
# OWEN: Orus. THERAPIST: Jeremy Heartwell, Sadaltager. Directions are not spoken.

## Part 1

THERAPIST: What needs our attention first today?

OWEN: I wrote "mostly better" on the check-in.

THERAPIST: Does that still feel accurate?

[long pause]

OWEN: Not without Tuesday. I drank Tuesday and left it off the first page.

THERAPIST: Thank you for correcting it now. What do you know about the amount?

OWEN: Three cans. Then some whiskey. I measured the whiskey this time, but I'm not sure the can size was the same as before.

THERAPIST: We can preserve what you know without converting it into a certainty.

## Part 2

THERAPIST: What happened before Tuesday evening?

OWEN: A rejection email at four forty-eight. Then the laptop reminder at five.

THERAPIST: The same workday cue you noticed earlier.

OWEN: Yes. I turned it off and sat there anyway.

THERAPIST: Did you contact anyone?

OWEN: I started a text to my brother and deleted it. The library was closing early.

THERAPIST: Were any later-evening supports available?

OWEN: The specialist gave me a number. I told myself it was for worse nights.

## Part 3

THERAPIST: Did you drive or try to retrieve the keys?

OWEN: No driving. I did ask my brother when I could have them back.

THERAPIST: What did he say?

OWEN: That we should decide with the care team, not because I was annoyed.

THERAPIST: Where are the keys now?

OWEN: Still with him. My partner's spare is still with my partner.

THERAPIST: Any current withdrawal symptoms or acute illness?

OWEN: No. I contacted the medical service Wednesday morning and followed their instructions.

## Part 4

THERAPIST: You said two lower-use weeks before Tuesday. What does lower use mean here?

OWEN: Fewer evenings than before. Not none. I don't want "two sober weeks" written anywhere.

THERAPIST: I won't write that. What made Tuesday hard to include?

OWEN: I wanted the map visit and the radio to mean I'd moved on.

THERAPIST: Did Tuesday erase those actions?

OWEN: No. It made them feel smaller.

THERAPIST: Smaller is different from unreal.

OWEN: I can accept that sentence today.

## Part 5

THERAPIST: What did the specialist appointment add to the plan?

OWEN: More frequent check-ins for now and a group orientation. I agreed to attend once before deciding what I think.

THERAPIST: Is the orientation completed or scheduled?

OWEN: Scheduled for Monday. Not completed.

THERAPIST: Anything about the plan you do not want shared here?

OWEN: The medical details stay with them. You can record that care is coordinated and that I consented to contact within the signed limits.

THERAPIST: We will follow those limits.

OWEN: I brought your form back today.

## Part 6

THERAPIST: Any thoughts of harming yourself or wanting to die around Tuesday?

OWEN: I wanted the email and the evening gone. No thoughts of killing myself, no plan, no steps.

THERAPIST: What will you do if the next five-o'clock cue lands that hard?

OWEN: Call the specialist number before deciding it is not bad enough. Go somewhere public if it is open. Tell my brother the rejection happened.

THERAPIST: What remains unresolved?

OWEN: Nights when places are closed. And asking for the keys back without pretending the risk is over.

THERAPIST: Those sound like two separate threads to keep visible.

OWEN: Yes. I don't want the corrected record to become a confession scene every time.

THERAPIST: It can be ordinary accuracy.
